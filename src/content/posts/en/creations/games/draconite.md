## A century later, something stirs

A hundred years ago, a meteorite fell upon the continent, bringing an energy source far ahead of its time: draconite. A new era began, but its story is far from over.

On the centennial of that arrival, the Sixth Draconite Technology Summit is about to begin. As a member of the Draconite Research Institute's Joint Security Force, you are responsible for keeping the event safe and orderly. Patrols, conversations, and requests should be routine work. Yet as scattered anomalies begin to connect, the Institute reveals another side.

This time, completing your assignment means finding answers for yourself.

## More than a patrol: people worth getting to know

Draconite is a **furry-themed side-scrolling adventure built around exploration and storytelling**. Its story unfolds through your actions—and through the details you take the time to notice.

- **Explore the Institute and watch for anomalies.** Interact with devices, documents, objects, and people to find extra information and crucial leads in ordinary places.
- **Follow assignments into a deeper investigation.** Patrol different areas, speak with staff, and take on requests. The quest system tracks objectives and story progress, but answers still depend on your own observations and actions.
- **Get to know people through their side stories.** Their requests offer a way into their histories, worries, and unspoken feelings, revealing new perspectives on the growing crisis.
- **Handle story challenges yourself.** Screen visitors, pass checks at critical moments, and respond to unexpected situations. Security work is part of the story, not just its backdrop.

As personal stories intertwine with scattered clues, the truth behind the summit draws closer.
